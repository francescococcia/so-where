# Pitch notes — broader scope

1. **Hook stays, then widens:** "Old Street. Clapham. Thursday, seven o'clock. Where do you meet?" … beat … "And on Saturday, when it's just you: what can I walk into right now?" One question, *so where?*, covers both. (Imaginer + Rebel)
2. **Differentiation line, broadened:** "Others find the middle. We tell you what's actually on, what will go wrong, and what we don't know." (Thinker: the Confirmed / Likely / Unknown chips are the proof)
3. **Harmoniser beat:** fairness ("24 vs 23 minutes") and privacy ("her area is rounded and never stored; you send the plan yourself").
4. **Persister beat:** the same honesty rules apply to every answer, whether it's a pub, a coffee or a free museum. Every claim has a source and a time.
5. **Promoter close:** "Date, coffee, or a free Saturday: type it, get three honest options, send the plan. So where?" Keep the business answer from the audit (B2B API / organiser tier, never paid placement).
