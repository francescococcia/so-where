# Encode London Hackathon 2026: event facts

Confirmed from venue slides and Luma. Times are London time: **BST (UTC+1) until Sat night; clocks go back at 02:00 Sun 25 Oct, so all Sunday times are GMT (UTC+0).**

## Where and when
- Encode Hub, 41 Pitfield St, London N1 6DA
- Fri 23 Oct to Sun 25 Oct 2026. Hacking started Fri 17:00. Event ends about Sun 16:30.

## Checkpoints (Encode Platform)
- [x] **CP1 Fri 18:00, check-in:** idea, bounty, Saint sign-up
- [x] **CP2 Sat 18:00, progress:** demo link (placeholder OK), GitHub link, what we're building and the problem. Entered for **The Real World** and **Agents That Ship**.
- [ ] **CP3 Sun 12:00, final submission:** demo video, code repo, our bounties. **No late entries.**

## Finale (Sun)
- 15:00 top 10 teams pitch on stage
- Audience votes live for the overall winners, then awards
- About 16:30 end

## Challenges and sponsor bounties
Our fit, criteria mapping and bounty choice: `knowledge/plan.md` §7–8.
1. **The Real World** (Encode): AI that understands the physical world from photos, audio, maps and open data.
2. **Agents That Ship** (Encode): an agent that does a real job end to end, with real tools, error recovery and human approval of risky steps.
3. **Google DeepMind, Gemini 4 Argon:** $7k / $5k / $3k in Gemini API credits.
4. **MongoDB Atlas:** prize TBA (Luma said £6,800 cash).
5. **Saint:** 10 teams only, sign-up closed Fri 18:00. Not available to us.

Sponsors and partners: Google DeepMind, MongoDB, Arc (Circle), Saint.work, Treg.
